2025 PHONE CASE GEN 1.0
Designed on 8/27/25

During college orientation, we were provided with our ID card and a dorm key. Since I am a very forgetful person, I decided to design a new phone case with a built-in retractable mechanism for my dorm key and a pouch for my ID and up to 4 credit cards.

2025 PHONE CASE GEN 1.1
Designed on 10/2/25

Update: After a few weeks of school, I realized that I need to swipe my ID card more often than I initially expected. With the previous design, I had to remove the entire phone case. I decided to design a hinge extension that allowed me to fold out and slide out my ID card without requiring me to remove the entire case.

2026 PHONE CASE GEN 2.0
Designed on 6/14/26

A complete redesign of the phone case for 2026, solving all the problems with the previous generation while making it a whole lot more complicated, for a good reason.
